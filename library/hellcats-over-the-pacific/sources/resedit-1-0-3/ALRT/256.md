## What this reveals about Hellcats

`ALRT` 256 and `DITL` 256 form a complete Macintosh alert definition. The `ALRT` provides window geometry and alert-stage information; the `DITL` provides the button and text.

For your strict Godot port, preserve this relationship rather than treating the DITL as an independent window. The original scenery filename substitution and the conditions that trigger the warning still require investigation of the executable.

The important result is that all 12 bytes of `ALRT` 256 are accounted for, and its connection to the previously decoded scenery error is explicitly established.
