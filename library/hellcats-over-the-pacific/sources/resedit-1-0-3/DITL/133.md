## What this reveals about Hellcats

This resource shows that the Mission Summary screen is built around several distinct information areas:

- Mission Awards: likely displays medals, commendations or other mission rewards.
- Targets Destroyed: likely summarizes completed objectives or destroyed ground targets.
- Total Aircraft Destroyed: a separate aircraft-related statistic.
- Score: displays the mission score.
- Skip Text, End Mission and OK: three distinct controls whose exact runtime behavior must be established from the game code.

Unlike the Refuel dialog (`DITL` 132), this interface contains no picture items. Six `userItem` regions are available for custom drawing, which suggests that significant parts of the mission summary may be rendered programmatically.

For your strict Godot port, the next step is tracing the routines that populate and draw these six custom regions, especially the scoring, mission-award and aircraft-destruction displays.
