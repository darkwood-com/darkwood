## What this reveals about Hellcats

The `High Scores` resource confirms a dedicated score display with four columns. Compared with `Dusty Roster` (`DITL` ID 130), the most notable difference is the additional Status column.

It also contains an `Erase Nonactive…` action. Its wording suggests a facility for removing inactive records, but the criteria for inactivity and the deletion behavior cannot be established from this resource alone.

For your strict Godot port, the next important step is tracing the routines that populate the large `userItem` region (item 10), format the score entries and handle the `Erase Nonactive…` action.
