## What this reveals about Hellcats

This resource provides direct evidence of two original copy-protection mechanisms:

1. Manual word verification: The player must consult a specific page, line and word in the F6F pilot's handbook.
2. Unlock-code alternative: The player could obtain an unlock code by returning a warranty card containing their serial number.

For your strict Godot port, the supplied data establishes the original instructions, positions and one word-entry field. The actual challenge generation, word validation and unlock-code verification require examination of the original executable code.
