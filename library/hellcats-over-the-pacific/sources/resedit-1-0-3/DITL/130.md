## Important observations for the strict Godot port

The resource establishes three distinct columns, ten text-entry controls, and the original positions of every visible item. It does not identify the actual pilot names, ranks, scores, initial focus state, or the behavior of the `Dossier…` button.

Also, the repeated `Static Text` strings are literal placeholders in the resource, not actual roster data. Their values may be replaced at runtime.

The next useful step is to inspect the original `DITL` ID 130 directly in ResEdit, particularly the single missing byte, and then trace the CODE routines that populate the roster and respond to `OK`, `Cancel`, and `Dossier…`.
