## What this reveals about Hellcats

The original game explicitly distinguishes saving the Settings from quitting the application.

The dialog presents three choices:

- Save: likely saves the current settings before quitting.
- Cancel: likely interrupts the quit operation.
- Don't Save: likely quits without saving settings changes.

These behaviors follow conventional Macintosh interface semantics but are not encoded directly in the `DITL`.
