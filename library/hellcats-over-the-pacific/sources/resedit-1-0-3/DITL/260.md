## What this reveals about Hellcats

The original game distinguishes between active-duty and non-active-duty high-score records.

This is particularly interesting when considered alongside `DITL` ID 130 (`Dusty Roster`), which provides a roster of pilots with rank, name and score fields.

It suggests that the game's pilot-roster system and its high-score persistence may be related, but the resource does not establish how pilots become inactive or how records are stored.
