## What this reveals about Hellcats

The Refuel interface is more complex than a simple confirmation window. It combines a large picture with configurable gameplay parameters.

The resource establishes that the player is presented with a fuel-related indicator and three bomb-load options, but it does not prove how the game updates fuel, reloads ammunition, or validates the selected loadout.

For your strict Godot port, the next useful resource is `PICT` ID 130. Recovering it directly from ResEdit on Mac OS 9 will reveal the original artwork occupying most of the dialog and clarify how the fuel and bomb controls were visually integrated.
