## What this reveals about Hellcats

The key discovery is that `About Hellcats` uses `PICT` ID 128 as a full-window background rather than constructing its entire interface from standard Macintosh controls.

The two additional `userItem` elements are regions whose appearance may be drawn by the application's code. Their exact role cannot be determined from this resource alone.

The third item is particularly noteworthy: its right coordinate is `533`, while the dialog is only `480` pixels wide. This discrepancy is present in the original bytes and should be preserved during reverse engineering rather than silently corrected.
