## What this reveals about Hellcats

Unlike the previous `DLOG` resources (IDs 127 and 128), this dialog has a meaningful embedded title: `Scenarios Dialog`, rather than the generic `New Dialog`.

The disabled `goAway` flag also distinguishes it from the two previous dialogs, suggesting that scenario selection is intended to be controlled through its interface elements rather than a standard window close box.
