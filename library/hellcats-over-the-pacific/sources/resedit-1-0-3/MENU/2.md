## Decoded `MENU` resource — ID 2

This classic Macintosh `MENU` resource defines the File menu of the original Hellcats Over the Pacific.

It contains 13 menu entries, including three separators, with keyboard shortcuts for opening files, starting missions, viewing the mission map, replaying gameplay, and quitting.

| `0x08–0x09` | `0000`      | Reserved          | 0            |

| `0x0E`      | `04`        | Title length      | 4            |

## What this reveals about Hellcats

The Instant Replay entry is especially interesting for your strict port. It provides direct evidence that the original game exposed a replay feature. Recovering that mechanism may require investigating simulation-state history, recorded controls, camera playback or other systems; the menu alone does not establish which approach the developers used.

The Mission Status, Mission Map, High Scores, and Save Settings entries also identify original interfaces that your Godot recreation will eventually need to reproduce.
