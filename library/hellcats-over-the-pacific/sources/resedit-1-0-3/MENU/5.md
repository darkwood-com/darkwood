## What this reveals about Hellcats

It establishes several original rendering controls:

- Five levels of scenery detail: these likely adjusted rendering complexity to accommodate different Macintosh hardware capabilities. Precisely which objects, distances or geometric details were affected remains unknown.
- Haze: suggests a distance or atmosphere-related rendering option, but does not identify the algorithm used.
- Clouds and Ground Clutter: establish distinct configurable scenery features.
- Shadows and Real Time Shading: show that the original interface exposed both options separately. We cannot yet determine whether shading was applied per polygon, per object or through another technique.
